## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a poised, reliable, and approachable digital interface engineered for modern web and application workspaces. It merges corporate authority with crisp, human-centered SaaS aesthetics. 

### Identity & Tone
- **Authority & Dependability:** Grounded deeply in rich corporate blues that evoke trust, institutional stability, and technical precision.
- **Warmth & Optimism:** Elevated with balanced warm amber accents that draw deliberate focus to key conversions and status changes without aggressive visual friction.
- **Clarity & Spatial Hygiene:** Generous breathing room, precise micro-spacing, and structural card modularity organize complex data into effortless visual hierarchies.

### Movement & Visual Language
The design system combines **Corporate Modern** rigor with **Tactile Soft Layering**:
- Structured card-based containers isolate content chunks to minimize cognitive fatigue.
- Borders remain understated and low-contrast, paired with tailored, deep ambient shadows that simulate physical depth and layered hierarchy.
- Interactive components feature prominent, softly radiused corners and smooth hover transitions, producing a tangible and tactile workspace.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around a progressive dark-to-light slate-blue spectrum paired with a dynamic amber-gold accent hierarchy for targeted interactions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Core (`#1E3A8A`):** The functional foundation for primary action triggers, key active navigation nodes, and highlighted links.
- **Primary Dark (`#17245F`):** Anchors high-emphasis headers, active interactive states (hover/pressed), and primary branding motifs.
- **Primary Light (`#EAF0FF`):** Tonal surface for active selection tokens, chip backgrounds, soft focus rings, and secondary primary button variants.
- **Accent Orange (`#F59E0B`):** Reserved for high-value conversion elements, notification pings, callouts, and system attention triggers.
- **Accent Light (`#FFF4D6`):** Low-contrast backing for highlight pills, alert banners, and active warning surfaces.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F9FC`):** The structural viewport foundation behind elevated components.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Base layer for cards, modals, dropdowns, sheets, and navigational sidebars.
- **Structural Border (`#E5E7EB`):** Subtle boundaries between canvas zones and internal card dividers.

### Text & Semantic Contrast
- **Text Primary (`#172033`):** Maximum contrast ratio for primary reading text, form labels, and critical headers.
- **Text Secondary (`#667085`):** Subtext, descriptive metadata, and unfocused form fields.
- **Text Muted (`#98A2B3`):** Placeholders, disabled states, auxiliary timestamps, and subtle framing elements.

## Typography

Inter serves as the unified typographic foundation across all viewports. Its tall x-height, neutral geometric forms, and meticulous screen legibility provide clarity in dense informational layouts while maintaining warmth across prominent headlines.

### Rules of Usage
- **Weight Pairing:** Reserve `700 (Bold)` strictly for display titles and hero banners. Use `600 (Semi-Bold)` for section headers, card titles, table headers, and functional button labels. Leverage `400 (Regular)` for standard paragraphs and data strings.
- **Optical Kerning & Leading:** Tighten letter spacing slightly (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) on headings larger than 20px to avoid loose tracking in modern screens. Maintain open tracking on `label-sm` to guarantee readability at glanceable scales.
- **Line Length Hierarchy:** Cap reading lines at 65–75 characters max to retain scan efficiency within card views.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is driven by an 8pt architectural rhythm set inside an elastic, 12-column responsive grid system. Content flows logically inside contained card decks designed to anchor dynamic SaaS workflows.

### Grid Geometry & Responsive Scales
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, centered. Side outer margins use `margin: 2rem` (or auto-centered), with a standard `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px) column gap.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) canvas margin. Complex split-panes compress into full-width stacked modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid structure. Gutters step down to `gutter-mobile: 1rem` (16px) with an edge margin of `margin-mobile: 1rem` (16px). Secondary card gutters collapse into unified vertical cascades.

### Layout Rhythm
- Internal card padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px) on desktop and gracefully falls back to `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports.
- Spacing between disparate dashboard modules and sections strictly employs `space-xl` (32px) or `space-2xl` (48px) to establish clean macro-whitespace.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through ambient, cool-tinted volumetric drop shadows combined with low-contrast structural boundaries. Surfaces do not rely on harsh black drop-shadows; instead, they draw from an indigo-infused shadow formula (`rgba(20, 30, 60, ...)`), producing an authentic optical depth that harmonizes with the blue foundation.

### Shadow Scale
- **Card Rest (`elevation-card`):** `0 4px 16px rgba(20, 30, 60, 0.08)`. Applied to interactive cards, persistent panels, and resting modular surfaces. Paired with a `1px solid #E5E7EB` border to frame high-key white backgrounds against the `#F7F9FC` background canvas.
- **Card Hover / Focused Interactive (`elevation-card-hover`):** `0 8px 24px rgba(20, 30, 60, 0.12)`. Applied during hover and drag states, shifting the element upward with a `translateY(-2px)` transitional leap.
- **Dropdown & Flyout (`elevation-menu`):** `0 12px 32px rgba(20, 30, 60, 0.14)`. Applied to context menus, select popovers, and date pickers.
- **Modal & Backdrop Overlays (`elevation-modal`):** `0 24px 48px rgba(20, 30, 60, 0.18)`. Floating surfaces requiring absolute spatial dominance over dimmed backdrops.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates intentional, varied corner radii tailored to element scale. The interface avoids cold sharp rectangles and overly pill-like bubbles, choosing friendly, modern geometry.

### Radius Hierarchy
- **Inputs & Interactive Controls (10px – 12px):** Form controls, text fields, and standard buttons utilize a calibrated `10px` to `12px` radius (`rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`), facilitating a clear, comfortable click target.
- **Cards & Data Modules (14px – 18px):** Primary UI cards, tables, split panels, and modals standardise on `16px` radius (scaling across `14px–18px` dynamically depending on container size).
- **Large Master Enclosures (20px – 24px):** Large hero cards, full-screen canvas envelopes, and standalone overlay containers adopt `20px` to `24px` radius (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`).
- **Tags & Status Pills:** Retain a full pill configuration (`9999px`) to maintain immediate differentiation from square-cornered actionable elements.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Button:** Background `#1E3A8A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `10px` (sm) or `12px` (md/lg). Padding: `10px 20px` (md). Hover state transitions to Primary Dark `#17245F` with subtle scale-up.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Background `transparent` or `#EAF0FF`, text `#1E3A8A`, 1px border `#1E3A8A` (or none if `#EAF0FF` fill).
- **Accent CTA Button:** Background `#F59E0B`, text `#172033` (or pure white depending on high-contrast setting), font-weight `600`. Hover darkens by 8%.

### Cards & Grouping Containers
- Built on `#FFFFFF` background, standard `16px` radius, enclosed with a `1px solid #E5E7EB` outline.
- Rest shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(20, 30, 60, 0.08)`.
- Hover shadow (when interactive): `0 8px 24px rgba(20, 30, 60, 0.12)` with a `200ms ease-out` transition.
- Header, body, and footer segments inside cards are separated by structural margins or low-contrast divider borders (`#E5E7EB`).

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height: `44px` (touch-friendly baseline). Radius: `10px`.
- Border: `1px solid #E5E7EB`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#172033`. Placeholder: `#98A2B3`.
- Active Focus State: Border color switches to `#1E3A8A` accompanied by an ambient ring glow `0 0 0 3px #EAF0FF`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: `20px × 20px`, `6px` border-radius. Inactive border `#E5E7EB`. Active state is filled with `#1E3A8A` featuring a crisp `#FFFFFF` check icon.
- Radios: `20px` circular diameter. Active state: `#1E3A8A` outer rim with a centered inner dot.

### Chips, Pills & Badges
- Status badges use semantic pairings: Accent amber uses background `#FFF4D6` with text `#F59E0B` (or darker tone `#B45309` for WCAG AAA conformance); Neutral uses `#EAF0FF` with `#1E3A8A`.
- Fully rounded (`9999px`), padding `4px 12px`, typography `label-md`.

### Tables & Lists
- Table wrapper lives inside a `16px` radiused card. Header background `#F7F9FC` with text `#667085` (`label-md`). Rows alternate or rely on subtle `1px solid #E5E7EB` dividing rules, with full-row hover highlights using `#F7F9FC`.
